Azan Qibla Prayer Times

Accurate prayer times, azan alarms and the Qibla direction on your Android phone. Free, ad-free, and built to work without an account or an internet connection.

What the app does

Prayer times

Daily Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha times calculated on your device for your chosen city or current location, with a choice of Asr calculation method.

Azan alarms

Automatic azan at each prayer time, with full or short azan audio, and a simple mute switch. Alarms are rescheduled after a restart.

Qibla direction

A compass screen that points towards the Kaaba, using your saved location and your phone's motion sensors.

Home screen widget

See the next prayer and today's times at a glance without opening the app.

Hijri date and dark mode

Shows the Hijri date alongside the Gregorian date, with a light and dark theme to suit your preference.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no subscriptions, and no analytics. The developer does not collect or receive your personal data.

Privacy at a glance

  • The app does not collect, sell or share personal information.
  • Your location, city and settings stay on your device. They are never sent to the developer.
  • There are no advertisements, tracking tools or in-app purchases.
  • Read the full Privacy Policy for details and your choices.

Important note about prayer times

Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated estimates. They can differ slightly from your local mosque or authority, and the compass can be affected by nearby metal or magnets. Please confirm times with your local mosque if you need an exact timetable.
