1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Azan Qibla Prayer Times (the "app") handles information when you install and use it on an Android device. The app is developed and maintained by Tayyab Mehmood ("I", "me", "the developer"). By using the app you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

The app is designed to work on your device. Prayer times, the Hijri date and the Qibla direction are calculated locally, so the app does not need an account, a login or a server to function.

2. Information the app handles

The developer does not operate any server that receives data from the app. The table below lists everything the app reads or stores, and where it stays.

InformationWhy the app needs itWhere it is kept
Location you choose or allow: latitude, longitude, city name and time zone To calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and to schedule azan alarms for the right local time. Saved in the app's private storage on your device. Not sent to the developer.
City search text that you type To find your city. The app first searches its built-in list of cities on the device. Handled on the device. If no match is found, the text may be passed to Android's built-in geocoding service (see Third-party services). Not stored by the app.
App settings: azan on or off, azan audio style, Asr calculation method, dark mode To remember your preferences. Saved in the app's private storage on your device.
Motion sensor readings (accelerometer and magnetometer) To show the compass direction on the Qibla screen. Used live on the screen only. Not stored and not sent anywhere.
Error report: if the app hits an error, a short report with the time, the Android version, device information and the technical error details To show you what went wrong so you can choose to report it. Saved on your device. It is never sent automatically. Only if you tap Copy or Email and send it yourself does it leave your device.

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising identifier, device identifiers for tracking, or usage analytics.

3. How the information is used

  • To calculate and display daily prayer times and the Hijri date for your location.
  • To schedule and play the azan at the correct times, including after your phone restarts.
  • To show the next prayer and today's times on the home screen widget.
  • To point the Qibla compass towards the Kaaba.
  • To remember your settings between uses.

The information is not used for advertising, profiling, marketing, analytics or any purpose unrelated to the features above.

4. Location information

Location is the most sensitive information the app uses, so here is exactly what happens:

  • You can set your location by choosing a city, or by tapping the button that uses your current location. Allowing the location permission is optional; choosing a city manually works without it.
  • When you use your current location, the app reads it from Android's location services on your device. The device's location provider may itself use network or satellite services run by your phone's manufacturer or operating system vendor, under their own privacy policies.
  • The app saves the resulting coordinates, city name and time zone on your device so that prayer times and alarms keep working offline.
  • The app does not track your movements, does not run in the background to follow you, and does not transmit your location to the developer or to anyone else.
  • You can replace your saved location at any time from the app's settings, or remove it entirely by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

6. Third-party services

The app does not include any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social media software. The following outside services may be involved in using the app:

  • Android's geocoding service. If the city you type is not in the app's built-in list, the app asks the Android system to look it up. On most devices this service is provided by Google or the device manufacturer, so the text you typed may be sent to them to return coordinates. This is governed by their privacy policies, such as the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive that text or the result.
  • Google Play. If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information about your installation and device under its own policies. The developer does not receive your personal information from Google Play.
  • Your email app. If you tap the support email link or the Email button on an error report, your own email app opens. What you send is handled by your email provider.

7. Sharing and disclosure

The developer does not sell, rent, trade or share personal information. Because the developer does not receive or store your location, settings or error reports, there is nothing to disclose to advertisers, data brokers or other third parties. If I am contacted by email or phone, I will use the details you give only to reply to you.

8. Storage, retention and deletion

  • All app data is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage.
  • Data stays until you change it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all of it.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your saved location and error reports are not copied into device backups.
  • Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is no account to delete and no request to make for removal from a server.

9. Security

The app keeps your data in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot normally read, and it does not transmit your data to a server. This keeps the risk low. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security. Keeping your device updated and protected with a screen lock helps protect the data on it.

10. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age set by the law where you live). Because the app has no accounts, ads or data collection, there is no child's personal information held by the developer. If you believe a child has sent personal information to me, for example by email, please contact me and I will delete it.

11. Ads, subscriptions and purchases

  • The app is free to download and use.
  • The app is ad free.
  • No subscription is required and there are no in-app purchases.

12. Your choices and rights

You are in control of the information the app handles:

  • Access and correction: your saved location and settings are visible and editable inside the app.
  • Deletion: clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Permissions: allow or deny them at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Opt out: there is nothing to opt out of, as the app does not collect data for analytics, advertising or sale.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and similar laws may give you rights over your personal information, such as access, correction, deletion and objection. Since the developer does not collect or hold personal information from the app, there is generally no data to provide or erase. You can still contact me with any privacy question or request and I will respond.

15. Disclaimer

Prayer times, the Hijri date and the Qibla direction shown by the app are calculated estimates provided for convenience. They may differ from the timetable of your local mosque or religious authority, and the compass can be affected by nearby metal, magnets or an uncalibrated sensor. Azan alarms depend on your phone's settings, such as battery optimisation, exact alarm permission, volume and Do Not Disturb, and may not sound if the phone restricts them. Please confirm important times with your local mosque. The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, to the fullest extent permitted by law.
